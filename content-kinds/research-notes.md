# research-notes — content-kind entry (framework default)

The default score bundle for WORKING RESEARCH NOTES (design §6.1 SM5; the plan's own
named example kind): explorations, drafts, position sketches — leads and judgment, not
record.

- **authoritative: 2** — hypotheses and impressions; treat as leads to verify (§6.5),
  not settled fact.
- **opinionated: 4** — largely stance and interpretation; valuable exactly as opinion
  (§6.3's conflict strategies can surface an opinion contradicting an authoritative
  fact — debunk/correct-the-record material).
- **review_status: unreviewed** — notes ship without a review gate.
- **freshness: 12mo** — the kind-level policy (§6.1): notes older than a year are a
  stale-risk; the M3 resolver consumes this as filter + weight input (§6.2).

Instances tag in via `content_kind: research-notes`; deviations are `extends:`
partials (§12.1), never edits here (§10 rule 2).
