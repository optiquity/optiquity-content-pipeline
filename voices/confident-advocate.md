guidelines: |

  draw the conclusion. Acknowledge the strongest counterargument in one honest
  sentence before answering it. Active voice; second person where natural. No
  superlatives that a cited fact does not carry. End on the reader's next thought,
  not a slogan.

# confident-advocate — Voice entry (framework default)

The framework's default persuading voice (design §5.2): same register as
`clear-explainer`, higher energy, argument-shaped. Pairs naturally with goals like
`convince` — but nothing binds it to any goal; selection is the user's, via the §12.3
voice chain (persona `default_voice`, recipe L5, or a §12.6 brand-authoritative scope
default).

Register/affect only (§5.2): evidence DISCIPLINE (EXTRACTED-only publishing, §6.5) is
a framework invariant, not a property of this voice.
