# clear-explainer — Voice entry (framework default)

The framework's default explaining voice (design §5.2 — a named voice is a saved
slider configuration + guidelines): balanced register, low humor, steady tempo. It is
the L0 floor referenced by `personas/_schema.yaml` (`default_voice`) and the voices
half of the CA8 story — instances declare their OWN baseline voice at L2
(`instance/defaults.yaml`, §12.3); this entry is the honest generic beneath it.

Register/affect only (§5.2): what to assume vs. explain is the Persona's
`knowledge_level`, never this voice's job.
